# 30. P. Energy/thermal interpretation rules

Do not claim lower heat merely from lower wall time.

Do not claim lower energy merely from lower average power.

Interpret together:
Energy = power integrated over time.

A candidate may:
- run faster at higher power and still use less energy
- run slower at lower power and use less energy
- reduce energy but increase peak power
- reduce peak power but increase total energy

Report these tradeoffs explicitly.

For the product use case, prioritize:
1. energy per generated audio second
2. sustained thermal behavior
3. acceptable RTF
4. peak/average power
5. memory

Do not sacrifice audio quality or stability for a small benchmark-only win.
