12. IMPORTANT PRODUCT INTERPRETATION
==================================================

The product priority is:

- sustained thermal
- energy/playback-second
- memory
- acceptable RTF

Therefore a small amount of additional fragmentation is not automatically disqualifying if the candidate produces a clearly superior whole-device energy/thermal/memory result.

Likewise, a "cleaner" ANE topology is not automatically better if energy or thermal behavior worsens.

Execution topology is a mechanism/explanation metric, not the final product KPI.

14. IDENTIFY INTERNAL CPU ISLANDS IF THEY EXIST
==================================================

==================================================
15. DO NOT CHASE TINY GAPS
==================================================

Do not propose graph surgery for tiny trace gaps that are:

- near profiler resolution
- a very small fraction of prediction time
- not associated with meaningful energy/thermal cost
- not reproducible

The goal is not a visually perfect trace.

The goal is to find meaningful avoidable device-boundary overhead.
